## 📑 Key Research Contributions

- **Multiplex CRISPR Platform**: Designed a single-pot orthogonal Cas12a (DNA-targeting) and Cas13a (RNA-targeting) trans-cleavage reaction to detect *blaNDM-1* and *mcr-1* without cross-reactivity.
- **Visual Paper Biosensor**: Engineered a dual-test-line lateral flow strip providing high-contrast binary visual readouts within 50 minutes.
- **AI Signal Quantification**: Developed PyTorch MobileNetV3 computer vision backbones to quantify paper line depletion signals and estimate target concentrations down to attomolar limits (10 copies/μL).

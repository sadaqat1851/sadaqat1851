## 🔬 About Me

Welcome to my GitHub profile! I am an aspiring **Undergraduate Researcher** preparing to pursue my **BS in Bioscience & Biotechnology**, with a strong cross-disciplinary background in **molecular biology**, **data science**, and **AI-powered diagnostics**.

My research spans **CRISPR diagnostics (Cas12a & Cas13a)**, **isothermal RPA amplification**, **paper-based biosensors**, and **AI/computer vision** for environmental surveillance of **Antibiotic Resistance Genes (ARGs)**. I also explore **computational neuroscience**, **plant phenotyping**, and **bioinformatics** pipelines.

- 🏫 **Institution**: Uswa College Islamabad → Pursuing BS in Bioscience & Biotechnology
- 🎯 **Career Goal**: Research Scientist at the intersection of Molecular Biology & AI
- 📧 **Email**: [sadaqathussains507@gmail.com](mailto:sadaqathussains507@gmail.com)
- 🧬 **Primary Research**: CRISPR Diagnostics · Cell-Free Paper Biosensors · RPA · Point-of-Care Testing · Bio-AI
- 🧠 **Also Exploring**: Computational Neuroscience · EEG Signal Classification · Plant Disease AI
- 🚀 **Featured Project**: *Multiplex CRISPR-Based Paper Biosensor for Simultaneous Detection of Antibiotic Resistance Genes in Environmental Water Samples*
